# Slate project direction

The active application is `.`. Work solely in this build. `.local/legacy` is legacy research/reference; do not develop, build, or merge its UI. Read `PROJECT.md` and the active app's `DESIGN.md` before changes.

Slate combines Markdown knowledge work and paged handwriting to replace the owner's Obsidian and Flexcil workflows. Offline first, no account required. Preserve the established Slate design language, tokens, typography, and paper treatment.

Keep Windows, Linux, Android, and independent web behavior aligned. Persist user preferences and protect existing notes when opening folders or importing. Never claim editable Flexcil handwriting or synchronized audio without a verified decoder. See the active app's `docs/flexcil-import.md` for findings and limitations.
