# Slate project direction

The maintained application is at the repository root. Read `PROJECT.md` and `DESIGN.md` before changes. Legacy research is retained locally in `.local/legacy`; do not develop, build or merge its UI.

Slate combines Markdown knowledge work and paged handwriting to replace the owner's Obsidian and Flexcil workflows. Offline first, no account required. Preserve the established Slate design language, tokens, typography and paper treatment.

Keep Windows, Linux, Android and independent web behavior aligned. Persist user preferences and protect existing notes when opening folders or importing. Never claim editable Flexcil handwriting or synchronized audio without a verified decoder. See `docs/flexcil-import.md` for findings and limitations.

Run build and test commands from the root. `artifacts` is staging; completed runnable deliveries belong in `releases/<date-description>`. Keep dependencies, local backups, generated packages and private signing material out of Git.
