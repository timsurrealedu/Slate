# Slate workspace
Mode: Operate, with Read inside documents.
Design read: A student’s everyday writing and reading environment.
Concept spine: The open study desk. A quiet library gives way to a document without losing context.
Delivery tier: editorial.
Animation mode: interaction transitions only — subtle navigation and state changes, restraint and low latency.
No landing page, camera journey, promotional sections or autoplay.
Palette: light #f7f8fa, #eff0f3, #ffffff, foreground #253044, muted #59657a, ink accent #385775.
Dark: #191e27, #1e2530, #242c38, foreground #e6eaf0, muted #a3adbd, accent #a6c4e2.
Typography: DM Sans for the workspace; Source Serif 4 for sustained reading; a system monospace for syntax.
Corner grammar: 6px controls; 8px document previews; 12px floating surfaces. Pages remain square.
Workspace: persistent library on desktop, temporary library on narrow windows, touch navigation on phones.
Library: readable title, real document previews, folders and a compact document list.
Markdown: direct source editing with three modes and integrated document context.
Notebook: physical A4-proportioned pages, compact floating ink toolbar, thumbnails and unobtrusive paper selection.
Motion: 180ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) navigation and surface transitions. No animation on the ink path.
Assets: own Slate glyph, consistent functional icon family, self-hosted fonts and real document/page previews.
Primary actions: New note (Markdown or Notebook), Open vault, Import. Secondary choices appear on demand.
Quality: workflow-first, no dashboard metrics, no decorative panels, no forced onboarding.
The user explicitly delegates design and engineering decisions and asks not to stop for minor choices.

Build path: code-led. No approved comp or quality-board asset was used.
THESIS: A study workspace moves from a quiet library into a document while keeping its course, tabs and connections in reach.
OWN-WORLD: Cool pale surfaces, slate-blue ink, DM Sans controls, Source Serif 4 reading, square physical pages, fine separators and restrained floating controls. The Slate mark is three offset page strokes.
STORY: Return to a course, open a note or document, connect an idea, or begin a notebook from New note. Work is saved locally and can be backed up without an account.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A 228px desktop library rail, slim workspace bar, modest title, four recent document previews, folder row and a compact document list. On phones the rail becomes a temporary drawer and study destinations occupy a bottom bar.
FORM: Adaptive paged study workspace, selected directly under the user's explicit creative authority. No concept-roll seed exists; the user instructed "Make your own deliberate decisions" and "Do not stop to ask about minor design or engineering decisions." Do not fabricate a seed or comp approval.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
